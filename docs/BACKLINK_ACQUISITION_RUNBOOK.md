# Backlink Acquisition Runbook

## Purpose

Earn relevant, editorially placed inbound links to canonical Forever Lotus pages. This is an authority-building process, not a link-volume exercise. Do not buy links, use automated link exchanges, publish deceptive guest posts, or ask partners to link without a reader-serving reason.

## Baseline and comparison

1. Verify ownership of `https://foreverlotus.com` in [Bing Webmaster Tools](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/backlinks) and Google Search Console.
2. In Bing Webmaster Tools, record the referring domains, inbound pages, linked pages, anchor text, and any available comparison view for 3–5 relevant peer sites.
3. Export or record the snapshot monthly. Track both total referring domains and the subset that meets the quality screen below.
4. Treat tool counts as directional: crawlers discover links at different times, and a reported link is not necessarily a valuable endorsement.

## Quality screen

Prioritize a prospective referring domain when all of these are true:

1. Its audience genuinely overlaps with the destination page: research, education, humanitarian practice, ethics, stewardship, or responsible technology.
2. It has clear editorial ownership, a real author or institution, and original useful content.
3. The proposed placement is contextual, visible to readers, and not hidden in a footer, directory farm, or generated list.
4. The link can use a relevant, natural anchor—not a forced keyword.
5. The relationship and any sponsorship are disclosed; use `rel="sponsored"` or `rel="nofollow"` where appropriate.

Decline links from paid-placement networks, bulk directories, spun-content sites, unrelated coupon or casino pages, comment spam, and “guaranteed ranking” services.

## Weekly operating rhythm

1. Select one evergreen asset and one narrowly defined audience.
2. Research 10–20 prospective editorial contexts: course resource pages, research roundups, NGO toolkits, library guides, conference materials, respected newsletters, or practitioner publications.
3. Write individual notes identifying the recipient’s existing topic and the precise reader benefit. Never send bulk mail.
4. Offer a useful contribution: a factual correction, a source addition, an expert quote, a resource-page suggestion, or a pitch that matches the publication’s editorial remit.
5. Record the prospect, context, outreach date, response, requested destination, and outcome. Respect a non-response; one thoughtful follow-up is generally enough.
6. After a genuine placement goes live, verify that it resolves, is contextually relevant, and points to the intended canonical URL.

## Monthly review

Review:

- New referring domains and the proportion passing the quality screen.
- New links to each canonical destination, not only the homepage.
- Referral sessions, engaged sessions, and meaningful conversions from earned links.
- Anchor-text diversity and whether it reads naturally.
- Links lost, broken, redirected, or moved behind access barriers.
- Which resource topics earned replies, placements, and qualified traffic.

Publish no claim about authority growth until the source data and reporting period are clear. Use the [SEO Scorecard Template](SEO_SCORECARD_TEMPLATE.md) and [KPI Dashboard Spec](KPI_DASHBOARD_SPEC.md) to retain a decision trail.
