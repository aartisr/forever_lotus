Evaluator

A constructive test for alignment.

The Manifesto Evaluator translates seven commitments into 21+ criteria and returns principle-level feedback, a score, grade, and improvement checklist. It is a practical starting point for reflection, not automatic proof of organizational virtue.

Evidence standard

From claim to credible impact.

Baseline

Define measure, source, owner, frequency, and data-quality checks before intervention.

Independent review

Invite external examination of design and analysis rather than relying only on internal confidence.

Replication

Seek signals across independent contexts before treating an outcome as durable.

Major-claim threshold: baseline and post-intervention data, public method and limitations, third-party review, and durability through a reporting cycle.

Governance

Trust is a structure, not a tone.

The operating standards call for independent advice across humanitarian, research, and ethics domains; a defined ethics-review process; conflict disclosure and recusal; and clear decision rights for programs, public claims, and ethical exceptions.

Operations

Quality has a cadence.

01
Transparency

Versioned reports disclose methods, assumptions, risks, unresolved issues, and corrective action.

02
Reliability

Public systems need availability targets, severity definitions, response expectations, rollback readiness, and blameless learning.

03
Security

Dependency review, secrets hygiene, threat modeling, least privilege, and incident readiness are ongoing responsibilities.

04
Language quality

Localization should be testable, complete in critical journeys, and free from fallback leakage.

Long horizon

Nobel-caliber is a standard of work, not a title.

The phrase describes an aim: measurable suffering reduction, dignity and agency outcomes, independent validation, ethical governance, and adoption beyond a founding team. It never claims nomination, affiliation, endorsement, or an award.
