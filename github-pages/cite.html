Linking principles

Make every link earn its place.

Use the page that directly supports your point. Prefer a natural descriptive anchor over a forced keyword; preserve author context; and quote only with attribution. Do not use a copied summary when the canonical source is available.

Suggested citation

A concise, source-first format.

Forever Lotus. “Forever Lotus: A Moral Architecture for Conscious Creation.” https://foreverlotus.com. Accessed YYYY-MM-DD.

For research-specific context, attribute the Forever Lotus Research Dossier to Subasri Dorairaj and use the canonical research URL.

Partner standard

Editorial value, never link schemes.

Forever Lotus does not seek paid, automated, reciprocal, or context-free links. A reference is welcome only when it genuinely improves a course page, resource list, article, report, community guide, or reader’s understanding.
