Method

Evidence should clarify, not decorate.

The research protocol prioritizes DOI-indexed, peer-reviewed, and university-hosted sources. It preserves bibliographic information, labels primary texts separately, and asks authors to include limitations and access dates for changing web material.

Research anchors

What supports a public claim?

Claims about compassion, wellbeing, humanitarian practice, education, and peace require contemporary evidence. The dossier points readers to university research infrastructure and wellbeing research hubs, while rejecting anonymous summaries and AI-generated citations as primary evidence.

Claim discipline: A major public claim needs a traceable source, defined scope, and stated limitation. Scripture, history, interpretation, and measured outcomes must never be blurred together.
